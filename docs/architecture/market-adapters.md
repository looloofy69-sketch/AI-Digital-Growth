# Global intelligence and market adapters

## Decision

Use a **global intelligence core with independently validated market adapters**. We can research and benchmark globally and sell in more than one market. We must not assume that international benchmarks predict Algerian outcomes, or that any one market's results transfer to another.

## Shared global core

Share definitions and methods only when they are genuinely comparable:

- audit taxonomy, deterministic measurement definitions, evidence schema, and evaluation harness;
- common CRM lifecycle, cost accounting, project delivery, QA, incident, and release models;
- content-quality checks, analytics definitions, and experiment registry;
- reusable service kits and a decision log.

Every metric retains its source, coverage, timestamp, method/version, cohort and limitations. Avoid combining metrics that have different definitions or sampling frames without an explicit normalization method.

## Required market adapter

Each adapter is versioned and reviewed, with at least:

- market identifier, region, supported language(s), locale and timezone;
- addressable segments, exclusions, source catalogue, access method, terms and freshness;
- recipient type, channel, purpose and jurisdiction-specific policy rules, reviewed by qualified counsel where required;
- required disclosures, sender identity, suppression semantics, retention/deletion duties, and policy owner;
- currency, taxes/invoicing, payment methods, refunds/contracts and third-party cost treatment;
- offer-specific price bands supported by observed quotations, wins/losses and cost floors;
- reachable-account estimate, sampling method and source coverage;
- observed reply/meeting/win rates with denominators, sample size, cohort and uncertainty;
- delivery cost, labor, rework, support, gross margin, payment delay and renewal/churn;
- reviewed-on date, evidence links, legal status and re-review triggers.

## Market comparison

Compare candidate markets using reachable target accounts, problem severity, ability to prove findings, buyer access, willingness to pay, fully loaded contribution margin, sales-cycle length, competition, channel legality, payment friction, localization/support costs and retention evidence.

Label every input as **observed**, **externally benchmarked**, **estimated**, **hypothesized** or **unknown**. Legal and provider-policy failures are hard gates, not low scores to be averaged away.

## Launch rule

Research can be global immediately. A market may enter a commercial pilot only when a bounded offer, buyer-access method, price/cost model, contract/payment path, approved acquisition method, sample plan and stop/continue criteria are recorded. Multiple markets can be tested at once only when the added execution and analysis burden is budgeted. Report each market's cohort and P&L separately.
