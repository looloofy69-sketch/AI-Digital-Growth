# Initial operating gates

These are required operating rules, not claims that implementation or legal review is complete.

## Gate A — Commercial experiment

- Market/segment and sampling limits recorded.
- Offer scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, delivery estimate and cost floor defined.
- Source and acquisition channel reviewed for the intended market and recipient type.
- Prospect, audit and cost records preserve provenance.
- Stop/continue rules fixed before outcomes are examined.

## Gate B — External claim

- Direct observation is separated from inference.
- Evidence is reproducible, fresh and linked to the finding and tool/policy version.
- Independent verification is completed where required.
- Wording does not overstate severity or unsupported business impact.
- Required human approval is durably recorded.

## Gate C — Outbound submission

- Market/channel/recipient/source policy returns PASS for the exact use.
- Identity/source checks pass; global and applicable scoped suppression are checked at final submission.
- Every material claim is linked to verified evidence.
- Rate, budget and delivery-capacity checks pass.
- Approval and idempotency state are durable; provider outcome can be reconciled.
- Kill switch is clear.

## Gate D — Client delivery

- Scope and acceptance checks are agreed.
- Repeatable tests and independent QA pass for the change's risk.
- Preview/client acceptance is recorded where required.
- Release and rollback instructions exist.
- Handover, ownership, third-party costs, support terms, baseline and outcome metric are recorded.

## Gate E — Autonomy promotion

- Representative evaluation covers target markets, languages, sources and failure modes.
- Critical-error performance meets the risk-specific predeclared rule; sample size and uncertainty are reported.
- Human-review disagreement and calibration meet defined thresholds.
- Rollback/demotion is immediate and the known-good version can be restored.
- Accountable owner approves promotion; legal/compliance review is still required where applicable.

A public inbound check may operate autonomously only after security, privacy, abuse and release gates pass. Launch itself is not evidence for autonomy.
