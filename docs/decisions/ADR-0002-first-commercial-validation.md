# ADR-0002: Validate one paid workflow before building the full platform

- **Status:** Accepted as sequencing principle; offer/segment/market selection remains open.
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Context

The strategy spans multiple services, growth loops and automation capabilities. Building all of them before demonstrating paid demand creates cost and opportunity risk and makes failed outcomes difficult to attribute.

## Decision

Start with one bounded paid offer and the smallest end-to-end workflow needed to sell, deliver, measure and support it. Keep market and vertical choices evidence-driven, not pre-decided by subjective scores. Human-review external communications and consequential claims until policy and evaluation gates justify a narrower scope.

## Success evidence

A defined cohort of real prospects; paid pilot(s); measured acquisition effort and conversion denominators; direct labor and tool costs; scope/rework/support; accepted deliverables; and a client outcome metric with baseline and time window.

## Consequences

- Lower initial build cost and faster feedback.
- Some automation and multi-service breadth are intentionally deferred.
- The first offer may change after buyer evidence; the workflow should preserve reusable components without pretending the initial segment is validated.

## Revisit when

A repeatable offer has evidence of demand and contribution margin, or repeated buyer evidence invalidates the current offer.
